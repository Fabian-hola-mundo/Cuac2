# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three tiers of B2B clients, weighted equally: founders and startups who need a real brand identity for the first time; small-to-medium businesses that want to stop looking generic; and established companies that need high-end branding, editorial, or web work. The shared profile: someone who already knows what bad design looks like and is ready to invest in better. Spanish-speaking Colombian market is the core, with Latin American reach.

## Product Purpose

The Cuac Design website is the primary business card and conversion surface of a Colombian creative studio (est. 2024) offering branding, editorial design, illustration, motion graphics, and web design. Success looks like a prospective client arriving, immediately sensing craft and confidence, and reaching out to start a project.

## Brand Personality

Bold, editorial, confident. A studio that practices what it preaches: Colombian creative identity expressed with precision, not decoration. Direct, warm without being casual, and visually decisive. The site itself is the proof of the work.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: cream backgrounds, rounded hero cards, laptop mockups floating on gradients, purple accent-everything. This studio makes real things for real companies.
- Passive agency portfolios: endless white space, monotone grays, polite copy that never says anything, "we are a full-service agency" blankness. Cuac has a point of view.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site is the studio's best piece of work. Every typographic choice, spacing decision, and interaction is a portfolio item.
2. **Colombian identity as character, not costume.** Origin is expressed through voice, wit, and directness — not through icons of the Andes. It shows up in the writing and energy.
3. **Earned restraint.** Ember (#EC3813) earns its place at roughly 15% of any composition. Overusing the accent defeats it. Carbon and paper carry the weight.
4. **Type does the heavy lifting.** Fauna One and Manrope are the visual identity. Motion and decoration are secondary to what the type communicates and how it's set.
5. **Show work, cut pitch.** Claims ("we're passionate about design") are replaced by evidence and directness. Every section justifies its presence with something real.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a baseline. Key pairing #EC3813 on #151F28 and #151F28 on #F0F1F6 must pass AA contrast. Reduce motion support in all CSS animations. Spanish-language primary; no language-switching required currently.
